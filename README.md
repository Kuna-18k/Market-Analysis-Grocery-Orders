# 🛒 Market Analysis – Grocery Orders

### Customer Purchasing Behaviour & Product Performance

**Project Code:** CDACL-006
**Project Domain:** Data Analytics
**Tools & Technologies:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Excel

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing grocery order transaction data to understand customer purchasing behaviour, product performance, reorder patterns, and shopping trends.

The objective is to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights and actionable recommendations.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing behaviour.
- Identify frequently purchased products.
- Understand product reorder patterns.
- Evaluate shopping frequency and order timing.
- Identify popular product categories.
- Generate data-driven business recommendations.

## 📊 Key Business Insights

- Approximately 59.9% of purchased items were reordered.
- The average basket size was approximately 10.5 products.
- 10 AM was identified as the peak ordering hour.
- Produce and dairy eggs represented approximately 45% of items sold.
- Approximately 47% of orders occurred within seven days of the previous order.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Introduce targeted promotions during peak shopping hours.
- Encourage repeat purchases through subscription-based offers.
- Develop product bundles to increase average basket size.
- Use reorder patterns to improve customer retention.
- Optimize product marketing based on category performance.

## ⚠️ Data Limitations

The analysis uses the available source extracts. Some order-product records did not match the orders table, so certain analyses use a smaller matched dataset.
